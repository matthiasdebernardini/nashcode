Welcome to nashmeet 👋 — three quick steps

  1. Make sure you're on the tailnet — the nashcode viewer below should read “Reachable”, then pick a Repo.
  2. Grant microphone access — Chrome only lets this page ask, so do it once here.
  3. Sign in with Google and tick the calendars to auto-name speakers (optional).

Then add your name under “Who's on your mic”, hit Save, and click the nashmeet toolbar icon on any meeting tab to record.

Google Calendar

nashmeet reads the calendar event that overlaps each meeting to pre-fill speaker names. Read-only access, and only the calendars you pick.

Microphone access

nashmeet records your microphone alongside the meeting tab. Chrome only lets this settings page ask for mic permission — the background recorder can't — so grant it here once. Until you do, Start recording fails the moment it opens the mic.

Who's on your mic

When nashmeet hears voices on your microphone, it pre-checks these names in order — you first, then anyone sharing your webcam. Press Enter to add a name; click ✕ to remove.

nashcode

Where finished transcripts are filed. The viewer commits each one as markdown under context/meeting/YYYY/MM/ on the repo's default branch.
